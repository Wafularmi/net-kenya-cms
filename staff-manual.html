1. Introduction — The Lecturer / Trainer Role

Welcome to the {{SCHOOL_NAME}} Staff Portal. As a Lecturer or Trainer, you are on the front lines of the mission — teaching, mentoring, and equipping students for ministry. This manual guides you step-by-step through every task you will perform in the NET Foundation Kenya college management system.

What a Lecturer / Trainer Does

  • Prepare and publish lessons, study notes, and video lessons for your assigned courses
  • Mark class and chapel attendance registers
  • Build the question bank, create quizzes and CATs, and publish them to students
  • Schedule exams, register students, and organise seating
  • Mark submissions, enter scores, and monitor pass marks
  • Track syllabus coverage and student progress
  • Lead discussions and live classes, and support students via library, tickets, and messages

What You Cannot Do (Important Boundaries)

To protect student records and college finances, some areas are restricted:

  • Students: You can view students and class lists, but you cannot register, edit, or delete students. Only Admin and Assistant roles can add, edit, or delete student records. If a student record is wrong or missing, contact the administration.
  • Finance: You cannot view or touch the Finance section (fees, payments, receipts, payroll). Contact the finance office for any fee-related query.
  • Settings: You cannot change system Settings (programs, grading scales, terms, user roles). Contact the administrator for system changes.
Important: If you need a student added, corrected, or removed, or you need a finance or settings change, submit a support ticket or contact the administration — do not attempt workarounds.

Device & Browser Requirements

The portal works on phone, tablet, laptop, or desktop in any modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge) with an internet connection.

Bookmark this URL: https://netfoundation.ke for quick access.

2. Logging In & Out

Your Login Credentials

Your staff account is created for you by the administration. You will receive your login details from the college administration or your coordinator.

  • Username: Your phone number or staff email, as registered by the administration
  • Password: The temporary password issued to you (change it after first login — see Section 14)

Step-by-Step: How to Log In

Step 1: Open your browser and go to https://netfoundation.ke
Step 2: Wait for the login screen. You will see Username and Password fields plus a Sign In button.
Step 3: Type your username (phone number or staff email) into the Username field.
Step 4: Type your password into the Password field. Passwords are case-sensitive.
Step 5: Click "Sign In" (or press Enter).
Step 6: If correct, you enter the Staff Dashboard. If you see "Invalid credentials," re-check spelling and Caps Lock, then try again.
Important: Passwords are case-sensitive. Make sure Caps Lock is off. After several failed attempts your account may lock — contact the administration to unlock it.

First-Time Login — Accepting Terms

The first time you log in, a pop-up shows the college's Terms and Conditions. Read them, check "I agree," and click "Accept" to continue. You only see this once.

Forgot Your Password?

Contact the college administration to reset your password. You will receive a temporary password, which you should change immediately from your profile (Section 14).

How to Log Out

Step 1: Click your name or profile icon at the top-right corner.
Step 2: Click "Logout" or "Sign Out" from the dropdown.
Always log out on shared devices to protect student data.

3. Dashboard & Navigation

The Dashboard is the first screen after login. It summarises your teaching work at a glance.

What You Will See on the Dashboard

📊 Statistics Cards

Cards at the top show counts for your assigned courses, students, pending marking, quizzes/exams, and attendance. Click any card to jump to that section.

📝 To Do List

Pending marking, upcoming quizzes/CATs/exams, and unmarked attendance registers, sorted by date. The most urgent items appear first.

📅 Upcoming Events

Scheduled exams, live classes, CAT dates, and academic deadlines for your courses.

📢 Notifications

Alerts from administration, ticket updates, and student submissions. The bell icon at the top-right shows your unread count.

📚 My Courses

Quick links to the courses assigned to you so you can jump straight into content, attendance, or grading.

How to Navigate

Use the sidebar menu on the left to move between sections:

  • Dashboard — This home screen
  • 📚 Courses / Lessons — Your assigned courses and lesson content
  • 👥 Students — View students and class lists (view-only)
  • 📋 Attendance — Mark class and chapel registers
  • ❓ Question Bank — Create and manage questions
  • 📝 Quizzes / CATs — Create, publish, and monitor quizzes
  • 📄 Exams — Schedule exams, register students, seating
  • ✅ Grading — Mark submissions and enter scores
  • 📈 Progress / Coverage — Syllabus coverage and student progress
  • 🎓 Graduation — View graduation list (view-only)
  • 💬 Discussions / Live Classes — Forums and live sessions
  • 📖 Library — Digital books
  • 🎫 Support / 💌 Messages — Tickets and messaging
Note: You will not see Finance or Settings in your sidebar. Those areas are restricted to Admin and finance staff. If you need anything from those areas, contact the administration.

4. Viewing Students & Class Lists

You can view the students enrolled in your courses and print class lists, but you cannot add, edit, or delete student records. Student registration and edits are done by Admin / Assistant staff only.

How to View Your Students

Step 1: Click "👥 Students" in the sidebar.
Step 2: The list shows students linked to your assigned courses / study centre. Each row shows name, admission number, program, year, and study centre.
Step 3: Use the search bar to find a student by name or admission number.
Step 4: Use filters (program, year, study centre, course) to narrow the list to one class.
Step 5: Click a student name to open their academic profile: enrolled courses, attendance summary, quiz/exam registrations, and grades for your courses.

How to Open a Class List

Step 1: From Students, apply the filter for the course / class you teach.
Step 2: Confirm the total count matches your expected enrolment.
Step 3: Click "Print" or press Ctrl+P and choose "Save as PDF" to save the class list for marking or seating.
Important: The Add, Edit, and Delete buttons are not available to staff. If a student is missing from your list, has a wrong name, or left the course, contact the administration or submit a support ticket — do not create a duplicate record.
Download the class list at the start of term and keep a printed copy for physical registers and exam seating.

5. Lessons & Study Notes

Lessons are the teaching content students read and watch inside each course. As the lecturer, you create lessons, upload notes, order them logically, and attach video lessons.

How to Open Your Course Content

Step 1: Click "📚 Courses" in the sidebar.
Step 2: Click "View Course" on the course assigned to you.
Step 3: Open the "Lessons" or "Content" tab to see existing lessons in order.

How to Create a New Lesson

Step 1: Inside the course, click "Add Lesson" or "New Lesson".
Step 2: Enter the lesson title (e.g., "Lesson 3: The Person of Christ").
Step 3: Type or paste the lesson content / study notes into the editor. Use headings, bold, lists, and scripture references for readability.
Step 4: Set the lesson order number (1, 2, 3…) so lessons appear in teaching sequence.
Step 5: Click "Save". The lesson appears in the course outline.

How to Upload Notes (PDF / Slides / Documents)

Step 1: Open the lesson for editing (click "Edit" next to the lesson).
Step 2: Find the Attachments or Upload Notes section.
Step 3: Click "Choose File" and select the PDF, Word, or PowerPoint file from your device.
Step 4: Wait for the upload to finish, then click "Save". Students will see a download link inside the lesson.
Keep file names clear, e.g., "BTH101-Lesson3-Notes.pdf". Keep files under the recommended size limit so students on phones can download them.

How to Add a Video Lesson

Step 1: Open the lesson for editing.
Step 2: Paste the video link (e.g., YouTube) into the Video URL field, or upload a video file if the system allows.
Step 3: Save the lesson. Students will see an embedded player inside the lesson.

How to Order / Reorder Lessons

Step 1: In the Lessons tab, find the order numbers or drag handles.
Step 2: Change the order numbers or drag lessons into teaching sequence (Lesson 1 first).
Step 3: Click "Save Order" if required. Open the student preview to confirm the sequence is correct.

Editing and Publishing

Step 1: Click "Edit" to update lesson text, replace attachments, or fix errors, then "Save".
Step 2: If lessons have Draft / Published status, click "Publish" when the lesson is ready for students.
Note: Only publish lessons you have fully checked. Students are notified of new lessons, so avoid publishing incomplete drafts.

6. Attendance & Chapel

You are responsible for marking attendance registers for your classes, including chapel sessions where assigned. Mark registers promptly so records stay accurate.

How to Mark a Class Register

Step 1: Click "📋 Attendance" in the sidebar.
Step 2: Select the course / class, the date, and the session (e.g., Lecture 1, Morning).
Step 3: The class list loads. For each student, mark Present, Absent, or Late / Excused as provided.
Step 4: Double-check the totals (present + absent = total enrolled).
Step 5: Click "Submit" or "Save Attendance". A confirmation message appears.

How to Mark Chapel Attendance

Step 1: In Attendance, select Chapel as the session type and choose the date.
Step 2: Mark each student Present / Absent as above.
Step 3: Click "Submit".

Correcting a Mistake

Step 1: Reopen the same course, date, and session.
Step 2: Correct the student's status and click "Update" or "Save".
Step 3: If the register is locked, contact the administration to unlock or correct it.
Mark attendance on the same day. Late marking causes false low-attendance alerts (below 70%) for students.

7. Question Bank, Quizzes & CATs

Quizzes and CATs (Continuous Assessment Tests) come from your question bank. Build questions once, then assemble them into quizzes and publish to students.

How to Add Questions to the Question Bank

Step 1: Click "❓ Question Bank" in the sidebar.
Step 2: Click "Add Question" and select the course.
Step 3: Choose the question type: multiple choice, true/false, short answer, or essay.
Step 4: Type the question, enter the answer options, and mark the correct answer(s) and marks.
Step 5: Click "Save". Repeat until the bank covers the topic adequately.

How to Create a Quiz or CAT

Step 1: Click "📝 Quizzes" or "CATs" in the sidebar.
Step 2: Click "Create Quiz" / "New CAT".
Step 3: Enter the title, select the course, set total marks, time limit, open / due dates, and attempts allowed.
Step 4: Click "Add Questions" and select questions from the bank (or add new ones). Confirm the marks add up correctly.
Step 5: Click "Save". The quiz is saved as a draft.

How to Publish a Quiz

Step 1: Open the draft quiz and review questions, marks, time limit, and dates.
Step 2: Click "Publish". The quiz becomes visible to enrolled students from the open date.
Step 3: Confirm students can see it by checking the student preview or the registrations list.
Note: Once published and attempted, avoid editing questions or marks — it can distort scores. If a fix is essential, unpublish first or contact the administration.

Viewing Quiz Registrations / Attempts

Step 1: Open the quiz and click the "Registrations" or "Attempts" tab.
Step 2: See who started, submitted, or has not attempted. Follow up with students who have not attempted before the due date.
Set a clear due date and one attempt for CATs. Allow retakes only for practice quizzes, not formal CATs, unless the administration approves.

8. Exams — Scheduling, Registration & Seating

Exams are formal assessments on fixed dates. As a lecturer you schedule exams for your courses, register eligible students, and organise seating.

How to Schedule an Exam

Step 1: Click "📄 Exams" in the sidebar.
Step 2: Click "Schedule Exam" or "New Exam".
Step 3: Enter the exam title, select the course, and set date, start time, duration, venue, and total marks.
Step 4: Add instructions (what to bring, admission-number requirement, arrival time).
Step 5: Click "Save", then "Publish" so students can see and register for it.
Note: Confirm dates and venues with the administration before publishing to avoid clashes with other exams or chapel.

How to Register Students for an Exam

Step 1: Open the scheduled exam and click the "Registrations" or "Candidates" tab.
Step 2: Review the list of enrolled students eligible for this course.
Step 3: Tick the students to register (or click "Register All Eligible" if provided).
Step 4: Click "Save" or "Confirm Registration". Registered students show a "Registered" badge.

How to Organise Seating

Step 1: In the exam, open the "Seating" tab if available.
Step 2: Assign seat / desk numbers to each registered student (e.g., by admission-number order).
Step 3: Click "Save Seating", then print the seating plan and post it at the venue entrance on exam day.
Print the final registration list the day before the exam and carry a spare copy to the venue for verification.

9. Grading & Submissions

After quizzes, CATs, and exams, you mark submissions, enter scores, and confirm pass marks. Objective questions may auto-mark; essays and written work need manual marking.

How to Mark Submissions

Step 1: Click "✅ Grading" or open the quiz/exam and click the "Submissions" tab.
Step 2: Click a student submission to open their answers.
Step 3: For each manual question, award marks and optionally add feedback comments.
Step 4: Click "Save" or "Submit Marks". The total updates automatically.

How to Enter / Edit Scores

Step 1: In Grading, select the course and assessment.
Step 2: Type each student's score out of the total (e.g., 72/100).
Step 3: Click "Save Scores". Double-check totals before publishing results to students.

Pass Marks & Grading Scale

  • A (80–100%) — Excellent — GPA 4.0
  • B (70–79%) — Good — GPA 3.0
  • C (60–69%) — Satisfactory — GPA 2.0
  • D (50–59%) — Pass — GPA 1.0
  • F (Below 50%) — Fail — GPA 0.0
Important: The pass mark is 50%. Do not alter the grading scale — it is set in Settings by the administrator. If a score looks wrong after publishing, correct it promptly and inform the administration.
Mark promptly after the due date so students get feedback while the material is fresh, and the Dashboard To Do list clears.

10. Progress & Coverage

The Progress / Coverage screen shows how much of the syllabus you have taught and how students are performing. Check it weekly to stay on schedule.

How to Read the Coverage Screen

Step 1: Click "📈 Progress" or "Coverage" in the sidebar.
Step 2: Select your course. You will see lessons/topics with status: Covered, In Progress, or Not Started.
Step 3: Check the coverage percentage bar (e.g., 60% covered) and compare it with the term timetable.
Step 4: Review per-student progress: lessons completed, quizzes submitted, attendance rate, and average score.
Note: Students below 70% attendance or with missing CATs are flagged. Follow up with them early — before exams.

Manual Completion Overrides (If Permitted)

In some courses, a lesson taught physically (in class or at a study centre) may still show as incomplete online. If the administration permits, you may mark a manual completion override.

Step 1: On the Coverage screen, find the lesson/topic and click "Mark Complete" or "Override".
Step 2: Select whether it applies to the whole class or specific students, and add a brief reason (e.g., "Taught physically on 12/03/2026").
Step 3: Click "Confirm". The coverage percentage updates.
Important: Only use overrides for genuinely taught content. If you do not see the override button, your role does not have permission — contact the administration instead.

11. Graduation List

The Graduation List shows students who have met all requirements to graduate. It is view-only for lecturers — final approval is done by the administration.

How to View the Graduation List

Step 1: Click "🎓 Graduation" in the sidebar.
Step 2: Select the program and graduating year / cohort.
Step 3: The list shows eligible students with their program, GPA, and completion status.
Step 4: Search by name or admission number to confirm a specific student.
Note: You cannot add or remove names. If a student should (or should not) be on the list — e.g., missing grades or attendance — inform the administration with the details.

12. Discussions & Live Classes

Discussions and live classes keep students engaged between physical sessions. Moderate forums respectfully and run scheduled live sessions on time.

How to Participate in Discussions

Step 1: Click "💬 Discussions" in the sidebar.
Step 2: Select your course to open its forum thread.
Step 3: Read student posts. Click "Reply" to answer a question or guide the conversation.
Step 4: To start a new topic, click "New Post" or "Post a Question", enter a title and message, then "Submit".
Answer course questions within 48 hours where possible. Pin key announcements (exam dates, notes) to the top of the thread.

How to Run a Live Class

Step 1: Confirm the live class schedule (date, time, link) with the administration in advance.
Step 2: Open "Live Classes" or the Schedule tab in your course at the scheduled time.
Step 3: Click "Start" or the meeting link to open the live session (e.g., Zoom / Google Meet).
Step 4: Take attendance during or after the session (see Section 6).
Step 5: After the session, post a summary and any recording link in the course Discussions so absent students can catch up.

13. Library, Tickets & Messages

Use the Library to recommend reading, Tickets to request help, and Messages / Notifications to communicate.

How to Use the Library

Step 1: Click "📖 Library" in the sidebar.
Step 2: Browse or search by title or author.
Step 3: Click a book to read it in the browser or download it if allowed.
Step 4: Recommend specific books inside your lessons or discussion posts (e.g., "Read Chapter 4 of…").
Note: You cannot upload or delete library books — that is done by the administration. To suggest a new book, submit a support ticket with title and author.

How to Submit a Support Ticket

Step 1: Click "🎫 Support" or "Tickets" in the sidebar.
Step 2: Click "New Ticket".
Step 3: Choose a Category: Academic, Technical, Student Records, or General.
Step 4: Enter a subject and describe the issue in detail (course, student admission number, date, screenshots if any).
Step 5: Click "Submit" and track status (Open, In Progress, Resolved) from the Support page.

Messages & Notifications

Use 💌 Messages to communicate with students and administration. Click the 🔔 bell icon at the top-right to see unread notifications — ticket updates, submissions, and announcements. Click any notification to view details.

For urgent issues (e.g., exam-day problems), contact the administration directly by phone in addition to submitting a ticket.

14. Changing Your Own Password

Change your password regularly to keep staff and student data secure. You can only change your own password — not those of students or other staff.

Step-by-Step: How to Change Your Password

Step 1: Click your name or profile icon at the top-right, then click "Profile" or "My Account".
Step 2: Find the Change Password section.
Step 3: Enter your current password.
Step 4: Enter a new password and type it again to confirm.
Step 5: Click "Save" or "Update". A confirmation message appears.
Step 6: Log out and log back in with the new password to confirm it works.
Use a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols (at least 8 characters). Never share your staff password with students or colleagues.
Note: If you forget your password, you cannot reset it yourself — contact the administration for a reset and temporary password.

15. Troubleshooting — Common Problems

I Cannot Log In

  1. Check that Caps Lock is off. Passwords are case-sensitive.
  2. Confirm you are using the correct username (phone number or staff email as registered).
  3. Clear browser cache and cookies, then try again.
  4. Try a different browser or an incognito/private window.
  5. If locked out after failed attempts, contact the administration to unlock your account.

Students Are Missing From My Class List

  1. Check your course assignment and filters (program, year, study centre).
  2. Confirm with the administration that the students are enrolled in your course.
  3. Remember: you cannot add students yourself — only Admin / Assistant staff can register students.
  4. Submit a support ticket with the missing students' names and admission numbers.

Lesson Upload / Video Will Not Save

  1. Check your internet connection and file size (large files may fail on slow networks).
  2. Use supported formats (PDF for notes; standard video links for videos).
  3. Try a shorter file name without special characters.
  4. Save the lesson text first, then attach the file in a second edit.

Attendance Register Will Not Submit

  1. Ensure every student has a status (no blank rows).
  2. Check that the date and session are selected correctly.
  3. Refresh and re-submit; if the register is locked, contact the administration.

Quiz / Exam Scores Look Wrong

  1. Re-check the marks per question and total marks on the quiz/exam setup.
  2. Check for unmarked essay questions still pending manual marking.
  3. Correct the scores in Grading and re-save before students complain.
  4. If the grading scale looks wrong, do not change Settings — contact the administrator.

Page Shows Blank or Won't Load

  1. Press F5 or Ctrl+R (Windows) / Cmd+R (Mac) to refresh.
  2. Check your internet connection.
  3. Clear browser cache (Chrome: Settings → Privacy → Clear browsing data).
  4. Try a different device or network. If still down, it may be server maintenance — try again later.

Need More Help?

Submit a support ticket (Support → New Ticket) with details and screenshots, or contact the college administration / coordinator directly.
