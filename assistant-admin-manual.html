1. Introduction — Your Role as Assistant Administrator

Welcome to the {{SCHOOL_NAME}} Assistant Administrator role. You have admin-level powers, but only inside the tabs the main administrator toggles ON for you in Settings → Assistant Admin Access. Anything not toggled ON stays hidden from you.

How Your Permissions Work

Step 1: The main administrator opens Settings → Assistant Admin Access.
Step 2: For each tab (Students, Courses, Finance, Grades, Attendance, Coverage, Graduation, Certificates, Reports, Settings, and others), the admin switches your toggle ON or OFF.
Step 3: When you log in, your sidebar shows only the tabs toggled ON for you. Everything else is invisible to you.
Step 4: If your duties change, the admin adjusts the toggles — you do not need a new account. Log out and back in to see the change.
Important: Throughout this manual, every feature is described as "if assigned to you." Never assume you have a tab. If you cannot find a screen described here, it means that tab was not toggled ON for you — ask the main administrator.

What You May Be Asked to Do

  • Register and manage students, if the Students tab is assigned to you
  • Upload lessons and study notes, if the Courses tab is assigned to you
  • Mark attendance and chapel records, if the Attendance tab is assigned to you
  • Enter grades and manage exams and quizzes, if the Grades tab is assigned to you
  • Record payments, installments, and fee agreements, if the Finance tab is assigned to you
  • Generate letters and certificates, if the Certificates tab is assigned to you
  • View coverage, reports, and graduation lists, if those tabs are assigned to you
When in doubt, check your sidebar. Your sidebar is the truth about what you are allowed to do today.

2. Logging In & Out

Your Login Credentials

The main administrator creates your assistant administrator account for you. You will receive your login details from the administration.

  • Username: The phone number or username the admin registered for you
  • Password: The temporary password issued by the admin (change it after first login — see Section 13)

Step-by-Step: How to Log In

Step 1: Open your browser and go to https://netfoundation.ke
Step 2: Wait for the login screen. You will see Username and Password fields plus a Sign In button.
Step 3: Type your username (usually your phone number) into the Username field.
Step 4: Type your password into the Password field. Passwords are case-sensitive — watch Caps Lock.
Step 5: Click "Sign In" (or press Enter).
Step 6: If correct, you enter the portal as an Assistant Administrator and see only your assigned tabs. If you see "Invalid credentials," retype carefully and try again.

First-Time Login

The first time you log in you may see the college Terms and Conditions pop-up. Read it, check "I agree," and click "Accept." Then immediately change your temporary password (Section 13).

Forgot Your Password?

Only the main administrator can reset your password. Contact the admin office — you will receive a temporary password to use once, then set your own.

How to Log Out

Step 1: Click your name or profile icon at the top-right corner.
Step 2: Click "Logout" or "Sign Out" from the dropdown.
Important: Always log out on shared computers. Your account carries admin powers inside your assigned tabs — do not leave it open.

3. Your Dashboard & Navigation

Your dashboard is the first screen after login. It summarises what needs your attention — but only for areas assigned to you.

What You Will See

📊 Statistics Cards

Cards at the top show counts for students, courses, fees, attendance, and similar — limited to your assigned tabs. Click a card to jump to that section.

📝 To Do List

Pending items such as unpaid installments, unmarked attendance, ungraded quizzes, or upcoming exams, sorted by urgency.

📅 Upcoming Events

Exam dates, deadlines, and academic calendar items.

📢 Notifications

System alerts and ticket updates. The bell icon at the top-right shows your unread count.

Navigation — Only Assigned Tabs Appear

Use the sidebar menu on the left to move between sections. You will ONLY see tabs the admin toggled ON for you in Settings → Assistant Admin Access, for example:

  • Dashboard — This home screen (always visible)
  • Students — If assigned to you
  • Courses — If assigned to you
  • Attendance — If assigned to you
  • Grades / Exams / Quizzes — If assigned to you
  • Finance — If assigned to you
  • Coverage / Reports — If assigned to you
  • Graduation — If assigned to you
  • Certificates — If assigned to you
  • Settings — If assigned to you (most assistants do NOT get this)

What "Access Denied" Means

If you click something — or follow a link or bookmark — for a tab that is NOT assigned to you, you will see an "Access denied" message or the page will refuse to load. This is normal. It means:

  1. The admin did not toggle that tab ON for you.
  2. You are not doing anything wrong and nothing is broken.
  3. Do not try to work around it. Ask the main administrator to grant the tab if your duties require it, then log out and back in.
If a tab you had yesterday is gone today, the admin changed your toggles. Log out, log back in, and if it is still missing, confirm with the administrator.

4. Students — Register, Edit & Delete

If the Students tab is assigned to you, you may register new students, edit their records, and delete records when authorised. If the tab is not assigned to you, you will not see it at all.

How to Open the Students Screen

Step 1: Click "Students" in the sidebar (only visible if assigned to you).
Step 2: You will see the student list with search, filters (program, year, study center), and each student's admission number, phone, and balance.

How to Register a New Student

Step 1: On the Students page, click "Add Student" or "Register".
Step 2: Fill in the required fields carefully:
  • Full name — Exactly as it should appear on certificates
  • Phone number — This becomes the student's login username, so type it correctly
  • Program of study — Select from the dropdown
  • Year field — Enter the student's current year of study (e.g., Year 1, Year 2). This controls which courses, exams, and graduation rules apply to the student, so set it correctly at registration and update it on promotion.
  • Study center — Select the correct center
  • Date of birth, gender, email — Fill in where available
Step 3: Click "Save" or "Register". The system generates the admission number automatically (used as the student's initial password).
Step 4: Give the student their username (phone number) and password (admission number) and the portal address https://netfoundation.ke.
Important: Double-check the phone number and the Year field before saving. A wrong phone number locks the student out; a wrong Year puts them in the wrong classes and reports.

How to Edit a Student Record

Step 1: Find the student with search or filters.
Step 2: Click "Edit" or the student's name.
Step 3: Update the fields — including promoting the Year field when a student advances — then click "Save" or "Update".

How to Delete a Student Record

Step 1: Find the student in the list.
Step 2: Click "Delete" (you may need admin authorisation for this).
Step 3: Confirm the deletion when prompted. This is permanent — grades, fees, and attendance tied to the student may also be removed.
Important: Never delete a student to "fix" a mistake you can edit instead. When in doubt, ask the main administrator before deleting.

5. Courses, Lessons & Study Notes

If the Courses tab is assigned to you, you manage learning content: view courses, organise lessons, and upload study notes and materials. If the tab is not assigned to you, these screens stay hidden.

Viewing Courses

Step 1: Click "Courses" in the sidebar (only visible if assigned to you).
Step 2: Browse the course list. Each card shows course code, title, instructor, and credit hours.
Step 3: Click a course to open its lessons, materials, quizzes, and schedule.

Working with Lessons

Step 1: Inside a course, open the lessons list (in order).
Step 2: Click a lesson to view its study notes, videos, and downloads.
Step 3: If assigned to you, use "Add Lesson" or "Edit" to create or update a lesson: enter the title, arrange it in the correct order, and save.

Uploading Study Notes and Materials

Step 1: Open the lesson the material belongs to.
Step 2: Click "Upload", "Add Material", or "Add Note".
Step 3: Enter a clear title, paste or type the study-note text (or attach a PDF / slide file), and click "Save".
Step 4: Reopen the lesson as a check to confirm students will see the note correctly.
Use short, descriptive titles (e.g., "BTH101 Lesson 3 — The Doctrine of Scripture") so students and reports stay organised.
Important: Upload only authorised, lawful materials. Do not upload content you do not have permission to share.

6. Attendance & Chapel Records

If the Attendance tab is assigned to you, you mark class attendance and chapel records that feed reports and graduation checks. If the tab is not assigned to you, you will not see it.

How to Mark Class Attendance

Step 1: Click "Attendance" in the sidebar (only visible if assigned to you).
Step 2: Select the course, date, and session.
Step 3: Tick Present / Absent (and Late / Excused where offered) for each student on the roster.
Step 4: Click "Save" or "Submit". The percentages and colour badges recalculate automatically.

Chapel Records

Step 1: In the Attendance area, open the Chapel session or register where offered.
Step 2: Mark each student present or absent for the chapel session, then save.
Step 3: Re-check totals after saving — chapel attendance may count toward conduct or graduation requirements.

Correcting a Mistake

Step 1: Reopen the same course, date, and session.
Step 2: Fix the ticks and save again. The record updates.
Mark attendance on the same day the class meets. Late marking causes forgotten names and disputes.

7. Grades, Exams & Quizzes

If the Grades / Exams / Quizzes tabs are assigned to you, you enter scores, organise exams, and manage quizzes. Any tab not assigned to you stays hidden and shows "Access denied" if you try to open it directly.

Entering and Managing Grades

Step 1: Click "Grades" in the sidebar (only visible if assigned to you).
Step 2: Select the course and assessment (assignment, quiz, or exam).
Step 3: Enter each student's numerical score (out of 100), then save. Letter grades and GPA compute automatically:
  • A (80–100%) — Excellent — GPA 4.0
  • B (70–79%) — Good — GPA 3.0
  • C (60–69%) — Satisfactory — GPA 2.0
  • D (50–59%) — Pass — GPA 1.0
  • F (Below 50%) — Fail — GPA 0.0
Step 4: Publish or confirm the grades where the system requires it so students can see them.

Managing Exams

Step 1: Click "Exams" in the sidebar (only visible if assigned to you).
Step 2: View Upcoming and Past lists. Each entry shows title, course, date, time, and venue.
Step 3: If assigned to you, use "Create Exam" to schedule a new exam: enter title, course, date, time, and venue, then save.
Step 4: Review student registrations (green "Registered" badges) and handle retake requests as directed by the administrator.

Managing Quizzes

Step 1: Click "Quizzes" in the sidebar or open the Quizzes tab inside a course (only visible if assigned to you).
Step 2: Create or edit a quiz: set the title, questions, marks, and time limit, then save and attach it to the correct lesson or course.
Step 3: Monitor attempts and scores; reset an attempt only when authorised.
Important: Grades are official records. Enter them carefully, double-check before publishing, and never alter a score without authorisation.

8. Finance You Can Touch

If the Finance tab is assigned to you, you handle day-to-day money records: payments, installments, fee agreements, and reading M-Pesa status. If the Finance tab is not assigned to you, you cannot see any of this.

How to Record a Payment

Step 1: Click "Finance" in the sidebar (only visible if assigned to you).
Step 2: Find the student by name, admission number, or phone.
Step 3: Click "Record Payment" or "Add Payment".
Step 4: Enter the amount, date, payment method (M-Pesa, bank, cash), and receipt or transaction reference exactly as on the proof of payment.
Step 5: Click "Save". The student's Total Paid, Balance, and payment history update immediately.
Important: Never record a payment without proof (M-Pesa message, bank slip, or signed cash receipt). Type the receipt number exactly — it is the audit trail.

Installments and Fee Agreements

Step 1: Open the student's finance record.
Step 2: To set up a plan, click "Installments" or "Fee Agreement" and enter each installment's amount and due date, then save.
Step 3: As the student pays, record each payment against the plan. The system marks installments paid, pending, or overdue.
Step 4: If an agreement changes, update the amounts and dates (only when authorised) and keep a written record of who approved the change.

Reading M-Pesa Status

Where M-Pesa integration is shown, each transaction displays a status such as Completed, Pending, or Failed. A Completed status with a matching transaction code confirms the money arrived. Pending means wait and re-check — do not record it as paid until it shows Completed. Failed means no money moved — ask for a fresh payment.

For any fee dispute, show the payment history table (date, receipt number, amount, method) to the student before escalating to the finance office.

9. Fee-Gated Learning & Content Gates

Fee-gated learning means students with unpaid balances can be automatically locked out of lessons, quizzes, or exams until they pay. Your job is to understand what locked students see — you do NOT configure these gates unless the Settings tab is assigned to you.

What Locked Students See

  • A lock notice or banner on the course, lesson, quiz, or exam explaining that access requires fee payment
  • Their balance and what to pay shown on the Finance / My Fees screen
  • Normal access restored automatically once the qualifying payment is recorded

What You Should Do (Without Settings Access)

Step 1: When a student reports being locked, open their finance record (if the Finance tab is assigned to you) and check Total Fee, Total Paid, and Balance.
Step 2: If a qualifying payment is missing, record it properly (Section 8) and ask the student to refresh and retry.
Step 3: If the balance is fully paid but the lock remains, confirm the payment status (including M-Pesa Completed) and escalate to the main administrator — do not attempt to change gate rules yourself.
Important: Gate thresholds and rules are configured in Settings. Unless the Settings tab is assigned to you, you must never change them. Send gate-change requests to the main administrator.

10. Coverage Screen & Reports

If the Coverage and Reports tabs are assigned to you, you can monitor teaching progress and pull official reports. If either tab is not assigned to you, it stays hidden.

Using the Coverage Screen

Step 1: Click "Coverage" in the sidebar (only visible if assigned to you).
Step 2: Select the program, year, or study center to review.
Step 3: Read the coverage table: which lessons and topics are taught, in progress, or not started per course and center.
Step 4: Flag gaps (unstarted topics, stalled courses) to the administrator or the responsible trainer.

Pulling Reports

Step 1: Click "Reports" in the sidebar (only visible if assigned to you).
Step 2: Choose the report type: enrollment, attendance, grades, finance, or coverage.
Step 3: Set filters (date range, course, center, year) and click "Generate" or "View".
Step 4: To save a copy, press Ctrl+P (Windows) or Cmd+P (Mac) and choose "Save as PDF".
Always set the Year and center filters before exporting — unfiltered reports mix cohorts and confuse readers.

11. Graduation List

If the Graduation tab is assigned to you, you can review who qualifies to graduate. If the tab is not assigned to you, you will not see it.

How to Open the Graduation List

Step 1: Click "Graduation" in the sidebar (only visible if assigned to you).
Step 2: Select the program and year cohort to review.
Step 3: Each student row shows the checks that decide eligibility: required courses passed, minimum grades and GPA, attendance threshold, fee balance cleared, and chapel requirements where applied.

What the Statuses Mean

  • Eligible / Cleared — All checks pass; the student can graduate
  • Pending — One or more checks fail (failed course, low attendance, unpaid balance). Fix the underlying record (grade correction, fee payment, attendance fix) and re-check.
Step 4: Confirm or forward the final list exactly as the administrator instructs — do not add or remove names on your own.
Important: The graduation list drives certificates. Never mark a student eligible unless every check genuinely passes.

12. Certificates & Verification

If the Certificates tab is assigned to you, you generate letters and certificates and verify or reprint them. If the tab is not assigned to you, these tools stay hidden.

Generating Letters

Step 1: Click "Certificates" (or "Letters") in the sidebar (only visible if assigned to you).
Step 2: Choose the letter type (admission, recommendation, completion, or as directed).
Step 3: Find the student, confirm the name spelling and admission number, then click "Generate".
Step 4: Preview the letter, print or save it as PDF, and file a copy as instructed.

Generating Diplomas and Completion Certificates

Step 1: Confirm the student appears as eligible on the graduation list (if the Graduation tab is assigned to you) or as directed by the administrator.
Step 2: In the Certificates area, select Diploma or Completion Certificate.
Step 3: Verify the full name, program, year, and date, then click "Generate".
Step 4: Preview carefully — names and dates on certificates are permanent once printed and issued.

Verifying and Reprinting

Step 1: To verify a presented certificate, open the verification search, enter the certificate number or admission number, and confirm the holder's name, program, and issue date match.
Step 2: To reprint, find the original record and click "Reprint". Confirm the details are unchanged before issuing the new copy.
Important: Certificates are legal documents. Never generate one for an ineligible student and never alter names, dates, or grades without written authorisation.

13. Changing Your Own Password

Step 1: Click your name or profile icon at the top-right corner and open "Profile" or "My Account".
Step 2: Find the Change Password section.
Step 3: Enter your current password.
Step 4: Enter a new password and type it again to confirm.
Step 5: Click "Save" or "Update", then log out and log back in with the new password to confirm it works.
Use a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols. Never share your assistant administrator password with anyone — it carries admin powers inside your assigned tabs.

14. Troubleshooting — Common Problems

I Cannot Log In

  1. Check that Caps Lock is off. Passwords are case-sensitive.
  2. Confirm you are using the exact username the admin gave you (often your phone number).
  3. If the admin recently reset your password, use the new temporary password exactly, then change it.
  4. Clear browser cache and cookies, then try again — or try an incognito/private window.
  5. If still stuck, ask the main administrator for a password reset.

A Tab Described in This Manual Is Missing

  1. This is almost always permissions: the admin did not toggle that tab ON for you in Settings → Assistant Admin Access.
  2. Log out and back in to pick up any recent permission change.
  3. If it is still missing, ask the main administrator to check your toggles.

"Access Denied" When I Open a Page

  1. It means that tab is not assigned to you. Stop and do not try to work around it.
  2. Record what you were trying to do and ask the main administrator for access or for someone assigned to handle it.

A Student Cannot Log In

  1. If the Students tab is assigned to you, confirm the phone number (username) and admission number (password) in the student record.
  2. Check the Year field and enrollment — a wrong year or missing enrollment can confuse access.
  3. If the record looks right, ask the student to clear cache or try another browser, then escalate to the administrator if needed.

A Paid Student Is Still Locked Out of Content

  1. If the Finance tab is assigned to you, confirm the payment was recorded with the correct amount, date, and receipt, and that M-Pesa status shows Completed.
  2. Ask the student to refresh or log out and back in.
  3. If the lock persists, escalate to the main administrator — gate rules live in Settings, which most assistants cannot touch.

The Page Shows Blank or Won't Load

  1. Press F5 or Ctrl+R (Windows) / Cmd+R (Mac) to refresh.
  2. Check your internet connection by opening another website.
  3. Clear your browser cache (Chrome: Settings → Privacy → Clear browsing data).
  4. Try a different device or network. If it still fails, the server may be in maintenance — try again later.

Need More Help?

Contact the main administrator with the exact screen name, the student or record involved, what you clicked, and what message appeared (quote "Access denied" exactly where seen). A screenshot helps.
